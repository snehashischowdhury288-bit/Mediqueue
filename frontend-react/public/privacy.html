MediQueue Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

MediQueue ("we", "our", or "us") operates the MediQueue Hospital Outpatient Queue Management System. We are committed to protecting your privacy and healthcare information.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign up or schedule an OPD consultation through MediQueue, we collect:

  • Account Information: Name, Email address, and Phone number.
  • Queue Information: Assigned consultation token number, department, and doctor preference.
  • Authentication details: Google user identifier and email for Google Sign-In.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is solely used to manage OPD clinic queues, calculate wait times, and notify you when your consultation turn is approaching.

3. Data Protection & Security

All authentication records and appointment tokens are encrypted and stored securely using Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore. We do not sell or share personal information with third parties.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at org.novabeacon@gmail.com.
