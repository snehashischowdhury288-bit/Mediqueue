5-Patient Batching Engine

ACTIVE & ENFORCED

Patients are partitioned into discrete 5-patient OPD batches (Morning, Afternoon, Evening). Shifting occurs automatically upon completion or no-show.

Max Batch Size: 5 Patients

Priority Shifting Rule

ACTIVE & ENFORCED

Elderly, Pregnant, and Emergency patients are automatically inserted at the top of the active batch (Slot 1) without disturbing subsequent pre-allocated slots.

Priority Insertion: Slot 1 Shift

3-Turns Away Notification

ACTIVE & ENFORCED

When a patient's position reaches 3 patients ahead, an automated proactive notification is dispatched across In-App, SMS, and WhatsApp.

Alert Threshold: 3 Patients Away
